Individual CLA (Rewards and Funded Work) applies to the Corporation and its

## 2. Designated Contributors

The Corporation authorizes the individuals listed in Schedule A to contribute
on its behalf. The Corporation will notify EV in writing when an individual
should be added or removed.

Corporation is responsible for everything submitted through a listed agent
account. Section 11 of the Individual CLA applies, with the Responsible Person
as "You", and each agent account's profile or pull requests must disclose that
it is an AI agent.

employer, a university or research institution, or a funder, holds rights in the
Contributions that conflict with this license, and that any third-party code or
data in a Contribution is identified with its license.
