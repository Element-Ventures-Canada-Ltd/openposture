# Contributing

OpenPosture is part of the OpenChokepoint programme and uses its contribution terms:

- **CLA:** sign once, through the CLA bot, under the programme CLA (see the OpenChokepoint `CLA.md` and `CCLA.md`).
- **Scope:** supply-chain mapping and interface work only; no technical data on how components are designed, made, tested or perform (`CONTRIBUTION-SCOPE.md`).
- **Bounties:** recorded in the deferred ledger (`BOUNTIES.md`, `LEDGER.md`); claim by comment, assignment first.
- **Feedback:** `FEEDBACK.md`.

Good first areas: interface views, accessibility, the OpenChokepoint adapter, synthetic examples and tests. Do not propose scoring or ranking logic here; it is out of scope for the public tier.
