# Feedback, Ideas and Acknowledgement

This policy covers input to the project that is **not a Contribution**: ideas,
suggestions, critique, design comments, and bug reports, given in issues,
discussions, email, or anywhere else, without submitting work for merge. It
sets out how that input is used, how it is acknowledged, and what it does not
earn. Work submitted for merge is governed by CLA.md or CCLA.md instead.

## 1. Principles

- Feedback is welcome and is treated as public unless the giver says otherwise.
  Do not send confidential material.
- Credit is given accurately, with consent where it can be asked, and never
  overstated.
- Feedback does not create obligations in either direction. Credit is not a
  reward, a licence, or a promise.

## 2. Terms

1. **Not a Contribution.** Giving feedback needs no CLA and creates no
   employment, partnership, agency, confidentiality, or payment relationship.
2. **No reward.** Feedback alone earns no bounty or ledger entry. Only accepted
   work on a published bounty issue does (BOUNTIES.md).
3. **Use of feedback.** EV may use feedback, including in proprietary offerings,
   without obligation to the giver. To the extent the giver holds rights in it,
   the giver grants EV a non-exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide,
   royalty-free licence to use it, and EV owes no credit except as section 3
   provides.
4. **Ideas and expression.** Ideas are not themselves protected by copyright.
   The project writes its own expression and does not copy a person's text
   without permission or a signed CLA.
5. **No duty to respond or adopt.** The project may decline, defer, or ignore
   feedback without explanation.
6. **Not for security reports.** Vulnerabilities go through SECURITY.md.
   
## 3. Acknowledgement

| Level | When | How |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Thanks | Any useful feedback | A public reply or reaction in the thread, where one is appropriate |
| Credited | Feedback that materially shaped a merged change, a published document, or a decision | A commit trailer (`Suggested-by`, `Reported-by`, `Reviewed-by`, or `Tested-by`) and an entry in ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS.md |
| Contributor | The giver also submits work | Signs the CLA and follows the normal contribution rules; credited as a contributor |

Rules for crediting:

1. **Accuracy.** Describe what the feedback was and what it shaped. Do not say a
   person built, wrote, or implemented something they did not.
2. **Consent.** Ask before naming someone in ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS.md. If the person
   cannot be asked, credit by link to their public comment, not by name, unless
   they decline.
3. **Handle as given.** Credit the public handle or name the person uses. Do not
   look up or publish who is behind an account.
4. **AI-assisted and agent accounts.** Credit the account as it presents itself,
   and note "AI-assisted" if the account discloses it. Do not publish the
   identity of the person behind it.
5. **Withdrawal.** A person may ask for credit to be removed, and the maintainers
   will remove it going forward.
6. **Not more than credit.** Credit is not authorship, co-ownership,
   endorsement, a reward, or a promise of future work.

## 4. Record of receipt

Maintainers keep the public thread as the record of unsolicited input. When the
project's own work overlaps with feedback received, the maintainers record in
ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS.md the date received, the link, what it shaped (or "none"), the
credit level, and whether consent was asked. This protects both sides: it shows
what was received, when, and what was done with it.

## 5. Declining politely

A short, courteous reply is enough. For example:

> Thank you for the suggestion. We are not taking this forward at the moment,
> and we cannot offer a reward or commitment for feedback on its own. If you
> would like to contribute work, the CLA and contribution steps are in
> CONTRIBUTING.md.
