# Governance

This project, OpenPosture, is maintained by Element Ventures (Canada) Ltd. ("EV"),
exclusive licensee of its background IP. It is run as a steward-led consortium: EV stewards the project and
holds the inbound licence to contributions, and a Technical Steering Committee
("TSC") brings contributors, academics, and industry into planning. The
consortium is a collaboration framework. It is not a legal entity, partnership,
or joint venture, and membership creates no employment, agency, or equity
relationship.

## 1. Roles

| Role | Who | Authority |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Steward | EV | Owns the inbound licence (CLA.md, CCLA.md); appoints maintainers; final decision on licensing, trademarks, and the private core tier |
| Maintainers | Listed in MAINTAINERS.md | Review and merge pull requests; enforce HANDLING.md, CONTRIBUTION-SCOPE.md, and the Code of Conduct |
| TSC | See section 2 | Advises on open-tier roadmap, issue priorities, and bounty allocation |
| Contributors | Anyone who has accepted the CLA | Propose and submit changes |
| Corporate members | Organizations with a signed CCLA | Designate contributors (CCLA Schedule A); may nominate a TSC member |

## 2. Technical Steering Committee

- **Composition (up to 7):** EV (chair), one independent developer, one
  academic, one industry member, one domain-expert contributor, and up to two
  non-voting observer seats for public-sector or program partners.
- **Selection:** the steward appoints the TSC and publishes its composition by
  seat in MAINTAINERS.md. A member is named there with their consent; TSC
  working records are kept in EV's private repositories. Later seats go to
  contributors with accepted work, nominated by the TSC and confirmed by the
  steward.
- **Scope of authority:** open-tier roadmap, issue priority and labels,
  recommended bounty tier and allocation (see BOUNTIES.md), Code of Conduct
  escalations. The TSC has no
  authority over the private core tier, licence terms, or trademarks, but is
  consulted before any change to them.
- **Decisions:** by consensus where possible. Failing consensus, the chair
  decides and publishes a short written rationale with any recorded dissent.
- **Conflicts of interest:** members disclose any commercial interest in an
  issue and recuse from allocating bounty value to their own work.

## 3. This repository and EV's other components

This repository is licensed as set out in LICENSING.md (Apache-2.0 for code,
Open Government Licence – Canada 2.0 for documents and data) and accepts
contributions under the CLA. EV's proprietary components are maintained
privately and do not accept public contributions. Contributing here gives no
rights in them.

## 4. Commitments to contributors

These commitments limit how EV uses the relicensing right in CLA.md section 2:

1. Versions of the open tier already released under Apache-2.0 stay available
   to everyone who received them under Apache-2.0. EV will not withdraw or
   retroactively restrict them.
2. Any future change to the licence of the open tier applies only to versions
   released after the change, and is announced at least 90 days in advance.
3. EV may include contributions in proprietary or commercial offerings, as the
   CLA allows, but will not remove the Apache-2.0 release of those
   contributions from this repository.
4. Contributors are credited by name (or handle) for accepted work unless they
   ask otherwise.

## 5. Review of stewardship

Within 12 months of the first public release, the TSC reviews whether to move
stewardship of the open tier to a neutral foundation or similar body, and
publishes its recommendation. The steward decides, and explains the decision
publicly.
