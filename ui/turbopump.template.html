What a status means

  • No candidate found: no public record shows a company with the capability. These parts set the first interview agenda.
  • No Canadian-owned candidate: candidates exist, but every one is foreign-owned or a Canadian plant of a foreign parent.
  • Canadian-owned candidate: at least one candidate is Canadian-owned.

Ownership, not location

Ownership class is recorded separately from location. A foreign-owned plant in Canada counts as a Canadian subsidiary of a foreign parent, not as domestic.

Where ownership is unverified, the record says so.

What is left out

Turbopumps are on Canada's Export Control List (item 6-3). This map holds supply mapping only: part categories, companies and sources. No materials, processes, dimensions, performance, test data or lead times.

Sources and grades

Every entity cites an https source. Grades: confirmed (primary source) or reported (secondary). Citability: P primary, AN named analyst, AS secondary.
